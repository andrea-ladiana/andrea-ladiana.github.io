Lenia in a heat bath

Lenia is a cellular automaton that is continuous in space, time and state. From one smooth local rule, localised creatures arise that travel, split and react. We add a noise temperature ϑ and a probe field h, and ask the questions a statistical physicist asks of any object: how it responds, whether it is chaotic, how it dies and how long that takes. This page runs the model we study, with the parameters of our protocol, in your browser.

Four experiments of the project, run here with the protocol of the campaign and shown next to what the cluster measured. A creature in a bath is metastable: sooner or later a fluctuation makes it split, explode or fade. If the escape is a Kramers process, lifetimes are exponentially distributed and their mean grows as ⟨τ⟩ ≈ τ₀ eΔΦ/ϑ. Run independent replicas of the current creature at the current μ and ϑ, with the death criterion of E4, and compare with the cluster (128 replicas per point).

Survival

Arrhenius E4, five μ near the upper edge

The fluctuation–dissipation theorem ties the response of a system at equilibrium to its spontaneous fluctuations: ϑχ(t, tw) = C(t, t) − C(t, tw), a straight line of slope one in the plane of the two. Lenia is not a gradient dynamics, so the line is a question. This is experiment E2: at a fixed final time t*, for a ladder of earlier times tw, three replicas leave the state at tw with the same noise and static fields 0 and ∓(h/2)ε, where ε = ±1 is a random cut; their difference is the response χ, the unperturbed one gives the correlation C. The void at ϑ = 0.05 obeys the theorem in the continuum and calibrates the instrument; the Orbium shows what a travelling solid does.

Experiment E6: where does the Orbium's window end? The predicate Π(μ) asks whether the Orbium, relaxed at μ = 0.15 and brought to μ, is still one localised, moving creature with a steady mass after 2000 deterministic steps. Each edge is found by halving a bracket down to 5·10⁻⁴, then checked at a different sub-cell position: an end that changes answer marks the edge “unconfirmed”. Near the edge closest to 0.15, the branch m(μ) tells the kind of bifurcation: a saddle-node approaches its critical mass as |μ_c − μ|1/2.

At R = 13 the page retraces the campaign's bisection probe by probe and lands on the same edges.

    The model

    One step

    The world is an N × N torus of cells, each holding a value A(x) between 0 and 1. Every step each cell looks at a weighted average of A over a ring of radius R around itself, grows if that average is close to μ and decays otherwise:

    A←clip[0,1][A+dt(G(K∗A)+h)+2ϑdtξ],dt=1T

    with ξ independent standard Gaussian numbers, one per cell and per step. With ϑ = 0 and h = 0 this is Chan's rule (2019). Two letters must not be confused: T is Lenia's time resolution (steps per unit of time), ϑ the temperature of the bath. The factor √(2ϑ dt) is the Euler–Maruyama recipe that makes ϑ a temperature in the sense of the fluctuation–dissipation theorem.

    The ring and the bell

    K(x)∝β⌊Br⌋KC(Brmod1),r=|x|R,KC(r)=e4−1/(r(1−r)) G(u)=2exp(−(u−μ)22σ2)−1

    The kernel has B concentric rings of heights β1…βB, is normalised to unit sum, and is measured from the cell centre. A cell grows only if about μ of its ring is alive, give or take a few σ; the Orbium's values (μ = 0.15, σ = 0.015) are a very narrow taste, which is why creatures are rare and fragile.

    Two frames

    A travelling creature breaks the symmetry of translations without standing still. Compare the state with itself at an earlier anchor time tw:

    qlab=⟨A(t),A(tw)⟩‖A(t)‖‖A(tw)‖,qcm=maxd⟨A(t,·+d),A(tw)⟩‖A(t)‖‖A(tw)‖

    In the laboratory the overlap vanishes as soon as the creature has moved by its own size, as in a liquid; in its own moving frame it stays, as in a solid: a sliding solid. The best translation d is found below the cell, by Newton steps on the cross-correlation seen as a Fourier series. A maximum over translations is biased upwards even for unrelated fields (an extreme-value statistic), so the instrument also shows the same maximum against a random-phase copy of the anchor, with the same spectrum and no structure: small for a compact creature, large for a texture. The reading uses the 0.70 rule of our fluctuation–dissipation analysis (E2). The moving frame follows translations only, so a creature that rotates or pulsates loses its overlap in both frames.

    What the bath does

    Far from any creature K∗A ≈ 0, so G = −1: every empty cell loses dt = 0.1 per step, while a kick of the noise is √(2ϑ dt), 0.014 at ϑ = 10⁻³. The clip resets the rest to zero, and the void stays exactly empty at the temperatures a creature survives. At ϑ = 0.05 the kicks match the decay and each empty cell becomes a ball bouncing on a floor. That system obeys the fluctuation–dissipation theorem in the continuum,

    ϑχ(t,tw)=C(t,t)−C(t,tw),

    and is where we calibrated the instrument: at T = 10 it reads a 30% violation (a₁ = 1.30), which falls to 3% at T = 160. A violation measured on a creature at T = 10 has to be compared with that baseline.

    Escape and chaos

    A creature is a metastable state of the noisy dynamics. Lenia is not a gradient system, so instead of an energy there is a quasi-potential Φ, but the Kramers law still reads

    ⟨τ⟩≃τ0eΔΦ/ϑ,

    and the largest Lyapunov exponent comes from the exact linearisation of one step, with M = 0 on the cells the clip pins at 0 or 1:

    δA′=M⊙[δA+dtG′(U)⊙(K∗δA)],λ=limt→∞1tln‖δA(t)‖‖δA(0)‖

    A travelling creature has a free position, a neutral direction: its exponent should be zero. At R = 13 it is (λ = −0.002). On finer grids it is often weakly positive, erratically in R: the trace of the creature's motion through the lattice.

    About this implementation

    The engine (assets/js/core.js) is a line-by-line port of our Python package: the same kernels, growth functions, step order (growth, field, noise, clip), tangent map, circular centroid, components on the torus (8-connected, threshold 0.1, at least 5 cells) and death criterion. It runs in float64 with a mixed-radix FFT, so every torus size of the protocol is available (100, 104, 128, 208…). Against the Python code, a single step agrees to 10⁻¹⁵, the Orbium after 1000 steps to 10⁻¹⁴ and the co-moving overlap to 10⁻¹⁴, and the exponents, advances and speeds of the instruments match the campaign's. The labs are ports of the campaign's scripts: the reading of the FD plot gives E2's numbers and labels exactly on E2's own data, the bisection of E6 at R = 13 retraces the campaign probe by probe to the same edges, and the rheology of the void matches E5 to four digits. Lifetimes in the bath, with 128 replicas as on the cluster, fall within the 95% intervals of the E4 points we re-ran (for example 972 [814, 1170] steps against 956 [801, 1151] at μ = 0.1539, ϑ = 4.17·10⁻³). The random numbers are not numpy's: a seed reproduces a run on this page, not a run of the campaign, and a lab run with few replicas fluctuates as much as its interval says. The address bar always holds a link to what the simulator shows (creature, rule, bath, view); “Share link” copies it.
